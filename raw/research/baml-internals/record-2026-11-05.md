## Runtime schema and streams

FunctionSpec<Out>.output_type uses reflect.Type.of<Out> and validate_output_type; parse calls baml.sap.parse<Out>. VM package_baml/sap.rs extracts realized generic arguments, walks reached class/enum/alias definitions, builds CompiledSapModel and allocates typed heap objects. FromLlmOutput parses Shape and can reject; rejection excludes that candidate and can try another union member. This is runtime reification, not BEAML's proposed Schema dictionary ABI.

sap_model partial_default differs from missing_default. Incomplete strings can default empty, nullable fields null, containers empty; numbers/bools/multi-variant enums/nonnullable unions lack generic defaults. Complete missing nullable/container fields may default; required scalar omissions error. test_streaming.rs test_partial_uses_default_where_complete_would_error gives partial id=1,name="", while completed id-only fails. Shared T does not imply final validation or field readiness.

Stream next appends chunks; _parse_partial may return _NoYield. EOF caches final parse result; final drains/reuses it; cleanup explicitly says abandonment is not cancellation/successful parsing. Fixtures assert JSON-fence/prose skipping, final failure, cached outcomes, cleanup and spawned handoff. Assertions inspected, not run.

Union coercion uses variant order, score-zero early exit, prior array-element hints and best-score selection. This differs from canonical in-language union algebra and does not prove partial monotonicity or stable branch identity.
