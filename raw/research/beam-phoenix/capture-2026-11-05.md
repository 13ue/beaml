## Tagged source inspection

- [html_engine.ex](https://github.com/phoenixframework/phoenix_live_view/blob/v1.2.13/lib/phoenix_live_view/html_engine.ex): file compile macro reads source and passes file/line/caller/engine into TagEngine.

- [tag_engine/compiler.ex](https://github.com/phoenixframework/phoenix_live_view/blob/v1.2.13/lib/phoenix_live_view/tag_engine/compiler.ex): module has `@moduledoc false`; compilation splits static/dynamic parts and tracks changes.
