## What the plan got wrong

→ `~> 1.8.5` does not give Phoenix 1.8.5 (resolver took 1.8.16).
→ Flag set misses `--no-agents-md`: the generator writes an `AGENTS.md` into `app/`.
→ P5-04 misses the two compile-time signing salts (`endpoint.ex`, `config.exs`).
→ P5-07 `default now()` on `utc_datetime_usec` stores local time.
→ `createdb` in `db-up` is not needed; `ecto.create` does it.
→ A compile step after `ecto.create` hides warnings (order matters).
