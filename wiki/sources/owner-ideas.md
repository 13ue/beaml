## How a capture is made

1. The main agent writes the OWNER's statements as paraphrased `[Requirement]` or `[Statement]` lines into a new dated file under `raw/requirements/`, same day.
2. Each idea gets its register entry (`decisions-to-be-made.md`) or decision-log entry, and a line in the affected concept or planning page.
3. Contradictions with earlier captures or decisions go into a "Tensions" section of the capture and are listed in the register entry.
4. This page gets a row; `wiki/log.md` gets an entry.
